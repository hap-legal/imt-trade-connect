Pharmaceutical ingredients

Ingredient supply for drug manufacturing

Our second field of activity is the supply of raw materials and ingredients to licensed pharmaceutical manufacturing and formulation facilities. We supply manufacturers, never patients or consumers.

Ingredient supply is judged on documentation as much as on material. Specifications, certificates of analysis, batch traceability and manufacturing-site status are established before any quotation is issued.

Scope statement. Ingredients are supplied strictly to licensed manufacturing, formulation and laboratory facilities for use in their own production. IMT does not supply finished medicines, does not sell to the public, and holds no consumer-facing sales channel.

Active & functional materials

  • Active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs)
  • Intermediates and building blocks
  • Standardised botanical extracts
  • Vitamins, minerals and nutraceutical actives

Excipients & formulation aids

  • Binders, fillers and disintegrants
  • Solvents, carriers and emulsifiers
  • Preservatives and stabilisers
  • Coating and encapsulation materials

Documentation supplied

  • Certificate of analysis per batch
  • Technical and safety data sheets
  • Origin, manufacturing-site and traceability records
  • Pharmacopoeia reference where applicable

Category listings are indicative. Available materials, grades, pharmacopoeia compliance and minimum quantities are confirmed per enquiry against the buyer’s manufacturing licence.
