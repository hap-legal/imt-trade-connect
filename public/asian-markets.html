Asian markets

Built for importers and distributors across Asia

If you source from Europe, IMT reduces the distance — commercially, administratively and operationally.

Why partners in Asia work with us

Buying from European manufacturers at a distance introduces friction: supplier verification, documentation standards, language, incoterms and freight all take time to resolve.

IMT absorbs that work. We sit on the European side of the transaction as your counterpart, dealing with manufacturers directly and delivering a single, accountable point of contact.

  • One commercial contact instead of many suppliers
  • Documentation prepared for your destination market
  • Consolidated shipments where volumes allow
  • Communication in clear business English
