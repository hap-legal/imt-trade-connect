Import & export services

Trade handled from enquiry to delivery

We take responsibility for the operational middle of international trade so our partners can focus on their own market.

What we do

Six service areas

Import services

Product classification, import documentation, customs coordination and inbound delivery into Spain and the EU.

Export services

Export documentation, packing requirements, freight booking and delivery to Asian destinations.

Product sourcing

Manufacturer identification, capability assessment, quotation gathering and sample coordination.

Trade facilitation

Commercial intermediation: terms, incoterms, payment structure and order administration.

Logistics coordination

Route planning, consolidation, freight forwarding liaison and shipment tracking.

Market support

Guidance for Asian partners entering or expanding within European product categories.

Process

How an order progresses

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You submit a requirement through the contact form with product, market and volume detail.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    We confirm feasibility, identify sourcing options and flag any regulatory considerations.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    Written pricing with incoterms, lead time, packing and payment terms.

  4. 04

    Confirmation

    Order confirmed, production or allocation scheduled, documentation prepared.

  5. 05

    Shipment

    Freight arranged, customs coordinated, tracking shared through transit.

  6. 06

    Delivery & review

    Goods delivered, documentation closed out, next cycle planned.

Working with IMT

What partners can expect

We commit to

  • Written confirmation of every agreed term
  • Honest assessment of feasibility before quoting
  • Proactive notification if a timeline changes
  • Confidential handling of your requirements

We ask partners for

  • Clear product specifications and intended use
  • The destination market and applicable requirements
  • Realistic volume and timing expectations
  • A single point of contact for decisions
