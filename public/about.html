Our background

A family name, publicly established in 1985

IMT is a family-run trading company. The company name and logo were established publicly in 1985, and the values behind them — discretion, continuity and long-term relationships — still set how we trade today. We speak as a company rather than as individuals; that is deliberate.

The problem we solve has not changed either. European manufacturers often have products that suit Asian demand but lack a route to reach it. Asian importers identify European ranges they want to carry but struggle to verify suppliers and prepare documentation from a distance.

IMT takes commercial responsibility for the middle of that transaction — sourcing, verification, paperwork and shipment coordination. We are based in Spain, source across the EU, and work in international B2B English with every partner.

Mission & values

How we work

A predictable process

  1. 01

    Understand the requirement

    We start with the product, target market, volume expectation and timeline.

  2. 02

    Source and verify

    Suitable manufacturers are identified and assessed for capability and documentation.

  3. 03

    Quote and confirm

    Commercial terms, incoterms and lead times are set out in writing.

  4. 04

    Prepare documentation

    Export, import and product documentation is assembled for the destination market.

  5. 05

    Ship and coordinate

    Freight, customs and delivery are coordinated with progress updates.

  6. 06

    Follow up

    After delivery we review the outcome and prepare for repeat supply.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Who do you sell to?

Businesses only: European manufacturers, Asian distributors and importers, salon and wellness groups, and licensed pharmaceutical manufacturers.

Do you sell to consumers?

No. IMT has no consumer or retail channel, and nothing on this site is an offer of sale to the public.

Which regions do you cover?

Sourcing across Spain and the wider EU; commercial focus on South Korea, Japan, China, Singapore and the UAE and Gulf, with other Asian markets served on request.

How do enquiries work?

Send the product category, destination market, indicative volumes and timeline through the contact form. We reply to qualified B2B enquiries by email.

Can you support documentation?

Yes. Commercial, product, origin and batch documentation is assembled for the destination market before goods move. Requirements are confirmed per product and destination.

Want to know whether we can help?

Tell us about the product category and market you are working with, and we will tell you honestly whether IMT is the right partner.
