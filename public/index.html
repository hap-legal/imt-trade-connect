Who we are

A dependable bridge between European supply and Asian demand

IMT works with manufacturers, distributors, wellness groups and licensed pharmaceutical producers who need a trade partner that understands both sides of the route. Every relationship is business-to-business: we do not sell to end consumers and run no direct-to-public channel.

Our approach is deliberately unhurried: we verify suppliers, prepare documentation properly, and keep partners informed at each stage of a shipment. Reliability matters more to our clients than speed alone — so we build for both.

  • Supplier verification and product sourcing
  • Documentation and regulatory preparation support
  • Coordinated freight, customs and delivery

Our two fields

Beauty & wellness devices. Pharmaceutical ingredients.

Both are supplied strictly business-to-business — to salons, wellness groups, distributors and licensed manufacturers. IMT has no consumer sales channel.

Beauty & wellness devices

Non-surgical, non-invasive equipment for beauty salons, spas and wellness centres — facial platforms, light therapy, body care and salon consumables. We do not source surgical-grade or operating-theatre instruments.

Beauty & wellness devices

Pharmaceutical ingredients

Active ingredients, excipients and standardised extracts supplied to licensed drug manufacturing and formulation facilities, with certificates of analysis and batch traceability.
