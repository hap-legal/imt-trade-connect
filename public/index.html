Our background

A family business, bridging European supply and Asian demand

IMT is a family-run trading company. The name and logo were established publicly in 1985, and the way we work has stayed the same since: discretion, continuity, and relationships that outlast a single shipment.

Today we work with manufacturers, distributors, wellness groups and licensed pharmaceutical producers who need a counterpart that understands both ends of the route. Thorough over rushed — documentation before dispatch, and lead times communicated early.

  • Supplier verification and product sourcing
  • Documentation and regulatory preparation support
  • Coordinated freight, customs and delivery

Compliance & quality

Documentation prepared before goods move

Regulatory expectations differ by product category and destination. We prepare for them early rather than resolving them at the border.

Regulatory readiness

We confirm which technical documentation, declarations and registrations apply to the product and the destination market before supply is committed.

Supplier assessment

Manufacturers are reviewed for quality management practices and their ability to supply consistent documentation with each batch.

Quality control process

Inspection and verification steps are agreed in writing with the client before shipment, and confirmed per product and destination.
