Partners

Long-term relationships over single transactions

IMT works with a small number of committed partners on each side of the route. We are open to conversations with the following partner types.

Manufacturers (Europe)

European producers of medical devices or beauty products seeking a route into Asian markets without building an export function.

Distributors & importers (Asia)

Established distribution businesses looking for verified European supply with documentation prepared for their market.

Sourcing agents

Agents representing buyers who need a European counterpart able to qualify suppliers and manage shipment.

Retail & clinic groups

Buying groups seeking consistent supply of specific product categories on a recurring schedule.

Logistics providers

Freight forwarders and customs specialists working on Europe–Asia routes.

Regulatory consultants

Specialists supporting product registration and market entry in specific jurisdictions.
