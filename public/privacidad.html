Datos que tratamos / Data we process

Only the data you submit through the enquiry form: name, company, email, optional callback number, country, enquiry type, product focus, urgency and your message.

Finalidad / Purpose

To review and answer business-to-business enquiries and to maintain the related commercial correspondence. We do not sell consumer products and we do not use this data for consumer marketing.

Base jurídica / Legal basis

Your request and the pre-contractual or contractual relationship between businesses, and our legitimate interest in responding to commercial enquiries.

Conservación / Retention

Enquiry data is retained only for as long as needed to handle the request and to meet applicable legal obligations.

Destinatarios / Recipients

Data is not transferred to third parties except where required by law or where a service provider processes it strictly on our instructions.

Sus derechos / Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. Requests can be made through the contact form. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish supervisory authority (AEPD).
