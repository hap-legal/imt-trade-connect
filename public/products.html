Products

Two fields, handled properly

IMT trades in non-surgical beauty and wellness devices, and in ingredients for pharmaceutical manufacturing. Both are supplied business-to-business only.

Beauty & wellness devices

Professional, non-invasive equipment for beauty salons, spas, wellness centres and aesthetic studios, supplied with the technical documentation the destination market requires. Surgical and invasive surgical-grade devices are outside our scope.

View beauty & wellness devices

Pharmaceutical ingredients

APIs, excipients and standardised botanical extracts for licensed drug manufacturing and formulation facilities, released with certificates of analysis and full batch traceability.

View pharmaceutical ingredients

Business model. All supply is business-to-business. We sell to trade buyers, distributors, wellness businesses and licensed manufacturers — never to end consumers — and we run no retail or direct-to-public channel.

How supply works

What to expect when you enquire

Specification first

Share the product type, intended use and destination market. Precision here shortens everything that follows.

Sourcing options

We return suitable manufacturer options with indicative pricing and lead times.

Sampling

Where appropriate, samples are arranged before a first order is committed.

Documentation review

Required declarations and technical files are identified for the destination market.

Order and shipment

Terms are confirmed in writing and the shipment is coordinated end to end.

Repeat supply

Recurring orders are scheduled against agreed volumes and timelines.
