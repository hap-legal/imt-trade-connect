Cookies

What this site uses

This website is a static corporate site. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run third-party analytics.

Technically necessary

Only storage strictly necessary for the page to display correctly may be used. No profiling takes place.

Your choice

When you first open this site, a consent banner asks whether optional categories may be used. Nothing optional is activated before you agree, refusing is as easy as accepting, and your choice is recorded locally in your browser so the banner is not shown again.

  • Strictly necessary — required for page display, security and to remember your cookie choice. Always active; cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — remembers optional preferences such as display settings. Off unless you allow it.
  • Analytics — aggregated, non-advertising measurement. Not currently active; your choice applies if it is ever introduced.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time: open cookie settings. Clearing your browser storage also resets the choice.

Third-party resources

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record the request (including your IP address) as part of delivering the font files.

Changes

If analytics or other cookies are introduced in the future, this page will be updated and consent will be requested where legally required.
