Beauty & wellness devices

Non-surgical devices for beauty and wellness centres

We source and supply the professional, non-invasive equipment used daily in beauty salons, wellness centres, spas and aesthetic studios. We do not trade in surgical or invasive surgical-grade instruments.

Our device portfolio is defined by its setting: treatment rooms, not operating theatres. Every category below is professional-use, non-surgical equipment intended for trained beauty and wellness practitioners.

Scope statement. IMT supplies non-surgical, non-invasive beauty and wellness devices only. Surgical instruments, implantable devices and operating-theatre equipment fall outside our portfolio and we do not source them.

Facial & skin care technology

  • Multifunction facial platforms
  • Hydrodermabrasion and cleansing systems
  • Microcurrent and radiofrequency handpieces
  • Ultrasonic and galvanic skin devices

Light, thermal & body care

  • LED light therapy masks and panels
  • IPL and cosmetic hair-reduction systems
  • Cavitation and body contouring devices
  • Heat, cryo and massage-based body units

Salon, spa & consumables

  • Treatment beds, trolleys and lamps
  • Steamers, sterilising cabinets and UV units
  • Applicators, tips and single-use accessories
  • Retail-facing home-care device ranges for distributors

Category listings are indicative. Confirmed model lists, technical files, CE documentation and certifications are provided directly to qualified trade buyers under enquiry.
